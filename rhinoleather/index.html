3

Features

3.1 Wishlist and compare

Both are built into the Ella theme (§8), so there's no extra app or cost. With another theme they'd need an app or custom code, about 15h more. In Ella, the "Add to compare" button shows on desktop; on mobile, product cards show the wishlist button only.

3.2 Google reviews

We'd use Appio Google Reviews. It pulls your Google reviews in automatically and shows them as a widget on the homepage. The free plan shows 5 reviews; Premium (US$6.95/month) shows up to 1,000, removes the app branding and syncs twice a day. Setup is part of the build hours.

3.3 Filters

We set up filters with Shopify's free Search & Discovery app. They read from product data (size, colour, price, type), so the right fields are mapped during the migration (§5).

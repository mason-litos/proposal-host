What we'd improve

The mock-ups below show the direction. In Option 1 we build them with Ella's existing sections. In Option 2 we design every page in full first (§3).

2.1 Menu and header

TodayPaper → Rhodia → Stapled → Stapled A4 is four levels deep. Each level opens a new fly-out, which is hard to use and impossible on mobile.

Mock-upOne click on "Brands" opens all brands in A–Z columns, with a featured brand on the right. Top menu: Brands, Pens, Ink & Refills, Paper, Collectibles, Corporate Gifts, Engraving. Wishlist and account sit next to the cart.

Decision needed: Shopify menus go up to 3 levels deep, so the current 4-level structure has to be reorganised. We'll send a proposed menu tree; you confirm it before the build.

2.2 Homepage

Mock-upOne product row with tabs for "New Arrivals", "Best Sellers", "Sale" and "Specials". Each card shows the brand, price, a wishlist heart and "Free delivery in Singapore".

Mock-upA new "Find the right pen" section: Fountain pens, Ballpoint & rollerball, Ink & refills, Corporate gifts. Gives first-time visitors a clear way in.

Mock-upA brand logo grid linking to each brand's collection, plus "View all brands".

2.3 Footer

Mock-upA newsletter bar on top, then Shop, Help and Contact columns with WhatsApp, phone and email.

2.4 Collection page and filters

Mock-upFilters on the left: pen type, price range, nib size, finish (colour swatches), in stock and "Engraving available". We build these with Shopify's free Search & Discovery app.

Filters only work if each product carries the value (e.g. nib size "Fine"). If your Magento products already have these attributes, we map them during migration. If not, someone has to add them (§4, question 3).

2.5 Product page: engraving with font preview

Mock-upYour current engraving options, improved: a text field with a character limit, and each font button written in its own font, so the customer sees what they'll get before ordering.

2.6 Mobile

TodayThe notice, links, logo, search, cart and menu icon stack up and fill the screen before any product shows. The social share buttons sit on top of the content.

Mock-upA short announcement bar and one header row: menu, search, logo, account, cart. No floating share buttons.

5

Answers to your open questions

These are the questions you raised that were still unanswered. Our developers have now checked each one.

Q1 — Does Shopify charge an extra transaction fee on top of MultiSafepay's own fee?

Yes. When you use any third-party payment gateway instead of Shopify Payments, Shopify adds its own percentage on top of what the gateway charges you. The rate depends on your plan:

Shopify planMonthly (annual billing)Extra fee on third-party gateways
Basic$39 ($29)+2.0%
Grow$105 ($79)+1.0%
Advanced$399 ($299)+0.6%
This is worth doing the maths on before you choose a plan. The extra percentage applies to every order paid through MultiSafepay. At higher revenue, upgrading from Basic to Grow can pay for itself purely through the reduced surcharge — the plan fee difference is fixed, the percentage is not. We are happy to model this against your actual monthly turnover once you share a figure.

Q2 — Does Shopify automatically validate VAT numbers for B2B customers?

Not on a standard plan. Shopify calculates VAT correctly by shipping address, but automatic VAT-number validation (VIES lookup for B2B customers) is not part of the standard feature set — it requires either Shopify Plus B2B or a dedicated third-party app.

Question back to you: do you currently use a specific tool or app for B2B VAT number validation on your existing store? If yes, tell us which one and we will check its Shopify equivalent and include the setup. If you do not need automated validation, no cost applies. This is decision D3.

Q3 — Can prices show incl./excl. VAT next to the product price, exactly as in the three screenshots?

Yes, this can be displayed on the product page as you showed us. It is included in the quote at 8h.

The reason it carries hours rather than being a checkbox: the dual price display has to be configured correctly per market/country, not just dropped into the template. If your incl./excl. presentation needs to differ by market or country, the setup has to be built out for each one.

Question back to you: should the incl./excl. VAT display change depending on the customer's market/country, or is one single presentation enough for all customers? This is important — it directly affects the 8h figure. If multiple markets need distinct handling, the setup effort grows and we will requote that line before starting. This is decision D4.

Q4 — Can Shopify force fuel/battery items to ship by FedEx only?

Partially — and we want to be straight with you about the limit rather than discover it mid-build.

  • Works: if an order contains only the restricted products (fuel/batteries), we can configure Shipping Profiles so that just the one permitted method — FedEx — appears at checkout.
  • Cannot be guaranteed: if a customer puts restricted and normal products in the same order, Shopify calculates one combined shipping rate for the whole cart. In that mixed case we cannot reliably force FedEx-only at checkout. This is a platform behaviour, not something more build hours would solve cleanly.
Not included in the price below. We cannot give a reliable hour figure until we know how you want mixed carts handled (block them? warn the customer? split fulfilment?) and how many products are affected. Once you decide, we will quote this line separately and transparently. This is decision D5.

Q5 & Q6 — LOC/SKU on admin-only documents, and the full list of legacy custom fields

Both sit in the data migration workstream (LitExtension), not the store build:

  • Location (LOC) and Alternative (Alt) are migrated into Shopify metafields by LitExtension. Location appears only on internal/admin order documents and is never shown to customers; Alt appears only inside the product page and on no email. Because these fields are not rendered on the customer-facing storefront, they require no LitOS front-end work — and therefore add nothing to the price below.
  • You noted there may be further legacy fields beyond LOC and Alt, refined over years of use. Cataloguing those sits with LitExtension as part of migration discovery. If any of them later need to appear on the storefront, we will quote that separately — flagged in Section 8 as a known open risk rather than buried.
  • The "shipping table view" question is resolved: your screenshot confirmed it is an internal product/LOC/quantity listing, not a shipping rate table. It is the same data source as the custom fields above and migrates with them.

3D Truck Cap Configurator: Add to Cart Approach

When a customer clicks Add to cart, their configuration goes into the Shopify cart as a single line at the correct price. This approach works on your current Shopify plan.

October 12, 2026 · Based on configurator price list v1 · Prepared by LitOS / LitGroup

How it works

  1. The customer builds a cap

    The configurator calculates the total price, as it does today.

  2. It goes into the cart

    The system adds the variant of that cap model whose price equals the total.

  3. Choices travel with it

    Every option the customer picked is saved on the cart line and on the order.

Setup in Shopify

Each cap model is one product. Inside each product, every possible total price is a variant. With the current price list, each cap model has 140 price levels, which makes 840 variants in total. Shopify allows up to 2,048 variants per product, so there is plenty of room. These products are hidden from your storefront. Customers reach them only through the configurator.

Strengths and limits

  • ProOne line, correct price in the cart and at checkout.
  • ProNo Shopify Plus, no paid app and no separate server needed.
  • ProOptions are locked once the cap is in the cart. Changes go back through the configurator, so every cap is still configured there.
  • ProThe vehicle name sits in the line title. The full configuration is saved on the order and in order emails.
  • ProSales reports by cap model are available in Shopify as usual.
  • ProStandard features keep working: discount codes, Shop Pay and abandoned checkout emails.
  • LimitNo inventory tracking per option (side box, drawer, lock).
  • LimitDiscounts apply to the whole line. They cannot target a single option.
  • LimitPrices live in two places, the configurator and Shopify. Your team updates both whenever a price changes.
  • LimitShipping weight is set per cap model. Adding options does not change it.
  • LimitSold in USD only, unless the configurator is changed to show converted prices.

Safeguard: every new order is checked automatically. If the options recorded on an order do not match the amount the customer paid, the order is tagged and held for review before production.

We set up all products and variants once, from price list v1. After launch, your team keeps the configurator and Shopify in sync. If a total in the configurator has no matching variant in Shopify, that build cannot be added to the cart. Our store guide shows how to make each change.

A base price change is quick. An option price change is the hard one: it changes the possible totals of all 6 cap models, about 140 price levels each, and working them out by hand is easy to get wrong. To help, we hand over the spreadsheet we use to build the variants, so your team can recalculate the price levels from a new price list. For the long term, we recommend that W3D makes the configurator read prices from Shopify (see below), so prices are kept in one place only.

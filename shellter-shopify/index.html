3

"Build Your Cap": sending a build to the cart

The goal is that the customer finishes a build, clicks once, and lands in the Shopify cart with the right package and add-ons at the right total, without picking anything again. There are two ways to get there, depending on whether you already have a configurator you want to keep.

3.1 If you already have a configurator: what it needs

Your configurator already produces a SKU list and a total on its Review screen, so most of the work is done. To send that build to Shopify, it needs four things:

  1. The same SKUs as Shopify. Every SKU the configurator outputs must exist as a product or variant in Shopify with the identical SKU. Your team keeps these in sync when adding products, following our guide.
  2. Its "Add to cart" button must open a Shopify cart link. Shopify supports a cart link that holds several items at once, in the form your-store.com/cart/{variant_id}:1,{variant_id}:1,…. We'll show you where to find each variant ID in your Shopify admin, and the configurator builds this link from the customer's selection. This works even if the configurator runs on a different website.
  3. Shopify sets the price. The cart total is calculated from the prices stored in Shopify, not from the number on the configurator's screen. Both must come from the same price list.
  4. Optional: a build summary. The link can also carry a short summary of the build (for example, the color or a build ID), which then shows on the order so your team can see exactly what was configured.

On our side, we connect the Build Your Cap button and demo the hand-off with the demo products. The change to the configurator's own button is made by whoever maintains it. If that's you or your developer, we'll send the exact specification. If you'd like us to make the change, we'll need access to its code.

3.2 If you don't have one yet: Angle 3D Configurator

Angle 3D Configurator runs inside Shopify and links each choice directly to a Shopify variant, so the cart price is always correct. It's rated 5.0 from 93 reviews. Plans start at $39/mo (Basic: 10 products, 250 variants per product), then $69/mo (40 products) and $99/mo (100 products), each with a 14-day free trial. Two packages plus the add-ons should fit within Basic. The app shows your 3D models but doesn't make them. You supply the models, or Angle 3D's artists can create them for an extra fee.

Before we pick: tell us whether you already have a configurator you want to keep, or whether Angle 3D fits what you have in mind. If neither, send us a description or an example of the experience you want and we'll find the right app (§5, question 2).
4

California sales tax

We recommend Shopify Tax, which works out the correct California rate for each delivery address. Collection only starts after your California seller's permit is entered in Shopify. Shopify Tax is free for your first $100,000 in lifetime sales. After that, the fee is 0.35% of each order where tax is collected, up to $0.99 per order.

You set it up in your Shopify admin, and our guide walks you through it. To write that guide for your case, we need a few answers (§5, question 3). Do you collect only in California, or in other states as well? Do you have a permit number yet? Should shipping charges be taxed?

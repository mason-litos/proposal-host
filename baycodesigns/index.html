You asked for two options — replicate the current Squarespace site with the fixes you'd already flagged, or go further and redesign it. This proposal lays out both: the fixes in §2 are built into both options, and a dedicated design phase is the only thing that separates them.

1

Two ways to make the move

1.1 Both options include

  • Setting up the Shopify store, including choosing and configuring the theme
  • Navigation/menu setup
  • Full QA — cart, checkout and mobile — before launch

1.2 Option 1 — Replicate ($1,200, 40h)

Rebuild the current site's structure and pages on Shopify, including the practical fixes in §2 — header icons, submenu, typography, product card, footer, collection header and filters — built to our standard patterns. No separate design phase, so the shape of the site is new, but the visual language stays close to Ella's defaults.

1.3 Option 2 — Redesign ($1,800, 60h) — recommended

Everything in Option 1, plus a dedicated design phase (+20h) — a custom colour and typography system, header/submenu layout, and product card design, with a round of revisions — instead of our standard patterns. We'd recommend this one if you want the new site to look distinctly yours rather than off-the-shelf.

Who configures what

Payment and shipping are configured by you, in your own Shopify admin. We advise, document and verify — we don't enter the credentials.

This is deliberate. Connecting a payment provider means handling merchant credentials and API keys that are financial access to your business, and a shipping account carries your commercial rate agreement. Those belong with the account holder, not an agency. Everything else in this proposal is built by us as normal.

ItemYou doWe do
Payment providerOpen the merchant account, enter the credentials in Shopify admin, run the first live transactionRecommend the provider, supply a step-by-step setup guide, and verify checkout end to end afterwards
Shipping zones & ratesEnter the zones and rate tiers in Shopify admin, and keep them updated when your carrier revises pricesTurn your current shipping terms into an exact zone-and-rate sheet, walk you through entering it, then test real destinations and weights at checkout

This does not reduce the quote: the configuration time is replaced by time writing the guide and verifying the result afterwards.

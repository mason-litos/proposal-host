1

Proposed scope

1.1 Platform

Move the store at heliosupply.com from Shopify to WooCommerce (WordPress).

This proposal covers WooCommerce design & development only. Backend data migration (products, orders, customers) is not included — that's handled by a separate team, LitExtension (a sister brand), who provide their own estimate and quote once the volume to migrate is known.

The wholesale store at wholesale.heliosupply.com runs as a separate Shopify store and is not part of this proposal.

1.2 Theme

  • Option 1 — a free WooCommerce theme, built out with Elementor and styled to the current brand: deep navy palette, logo and typography. No theme license cost.
  • Option 2 — may move to a different theme better suited to the new design, if the redesign adds features or custom functionality.

1.4 Option 1 — Replicate & improve

Rebuilds the current site's pages and content on WooCommerce, keeping its clean, minimal look, with the following improvements:

1. Customer reviews

The "This is what our customers have to say" carousel runs on Klaviyo Reviews, which also supports WooCommerce. The carousel is set up on the new homepage from the same Klaviyo account, so existing reviews stay in place with no review data to move.

2. Contact page

The contact form moves off the homepage onto a dedicated Contact page, keeping the same fields (name, email, phone, order volume and hardware needs), built with Contact Form 7.

3. Product cards

A fuller card design than the current basic one, including an Add to Cart button directly on the card.

4. Featured Products section

A stronger visual treatment, so the homepage product section stands out instead of blending into the page.

5. Section titles

One consistent heading style across every section, including the product section.

6. Overall style

The current minimal layout works well and is kept. If a bolder look is preferred, the colour scheme and some section layouts are adjusted to match — confirmed before build.

1.5 Option 2 — Redesign

A new design for the store, not bound by the current layout — for a stronger, more distinctive look than a rebuild of the current site. The design (20h) is completed and approved before development starts.

  1. Visual direction — a fresh look built on the Helio Supply brand (logo, deep navy palette), with a bolder style: colour, typography and section layouts designed as one consistent system.
  2. Key templates designed — homepage, product listing (category) page and product page, for desktop and mobile. The remaining pages — Contact, content and policy pages — follow the same design system during build.
  3. Built for retail and bulk buyers — clear product categories, product cards with Add to Cart, customer reviews given prominence, and a clear route to the bulk-order enquiry form for smoke shops and dispensaries.
  4. Option 1 improvements included — product cards, the Featured Products section and consistent section titles (1.4) are resolved within the new design rather than as separate fixes.
  5. Same functionality — the plugins (1.3), Klaviyo reviews carousel, Contact page and age verification all carry over into the new design. The theme may move to one better suited to the design (1.2).

1.6 Checkout

Both options use WooCommerce's default checkout. Shopify's checkout is a fixed, hosted layout with little room for customization; WooCommerce's checkout is a page on the site itself and can be fully customized — so its default look differs from the current checkout.

A custom checkout closer to the current layout can be built, but it is not included in this proposal — it would be quoted separately on request.

1.7 Notes

  • Theme, Shopify subscription, payment gateway/shipping setup: standard scope notes — theme and Shopify plan are subscribed to/purchased directly by the client; payment gateway and shipping carrier setup handled by the client's own team for credential confidentiality (a setup guide can be provided).

Two ways to make the move

1.1 Both options include

  • Setting up the Shopify store, including choosing and configuring the theme
  • Navigation/menu setup
  • Full QA — personalization, cart, checkout and mobile — before launch

1.2 Option 1 — Replicate ($2,100, 70h)

Rebuild the current site's structure, pages and catalogue on Shopify, including the practical fixes in §2 — filters, mobile menu, product slider, shipping toggle, consistent colour, product card — built to our standard patterns. No separate design phase, so the shape of the site is new, but the visual language stays close to Ella's defaults.

1.3 Option 2 — Redesign ($2,700, 90h) — recommended

Everything in Option 1, plus a dedicated design phase (+20h) — a custom colour system, header layout, mobile menu style and product card design, with a round of revisions — instead of our standard patterns. We'd recommend this one if you want the new site to look distinctly yours rather than off-the-shelf.

3

What changes moving off WooCommerce

We went through the specific pages you flagged. Here's what each one needs on Shopify, and why — this applies to both options above.

3.1 Custom Orders & Custom Gun Stocks — forms with file upload

Shopify's native contact form can't take a file attachment, so both pages need a form-builder app. Two real candidates: POWR Form Builder (free tier: 25 submissions/mo; Pro $20.24/mo adds file upload) or Powerful Contact Form Builder (free tier includes file upload up to 2MB; Premium $14.90/mo raises the limit to 30MB). Final pick once we know roughly how many submissions a month to expect.

3.2 Laser Engraving Parts — same kind of form

Our read, different from the note: your note flags this one could run on the plain Shopify contact form instead of an app, since it's a smaller trade-off. But since the app from §3.1 is already running for the two forms above, we'd point this page at the same app too — no extra subscription, and customers don't have to email files separately. Tell us if you'd rather keep it on the free contact form instead.

3.3 Classic 2-Piece Turkey Plaque (and any other 4-option product)

Wood, Side Engraving, Engraved Image and Font — four option groups on one product. Shopify's native limit is three options per product, regardless of variant count, so this needs a product-options app: EasyFlow Product Options (free up to 20 products; Growth $9.99/mo for more products and file upload) or Easify Custom Product Options (free-forever tier with image/colour swatches; Pro $9.99/mo adds file upload). Either way, expect the picker to be close to — not a pixel match of — the one you have today.

Your current 4-option swatch picker on this product.

3.4 Request Product Information

The plain Shopify contact form covers this — no extra app needed.

3.5 Wishlist

Included natively if we build on Ella (§5), at no extra cost. On a different theme: a custom build (+10h) or a wishlist app instead.

3.6 Reviews

Install a review app — Judge.me (forever-free tier, or $15/mo) — to replace WooCommerce's native product reviews.

Who configures what

Payment and shipping are configured by you, in your own Shopify admin. We advise, document and verify — we don't enter the credentials.

This is deliberate. Connecting a payment provider means handling merchant credentials and API keys that are financial access to your business, and a shipping account carries your commercial rate agreement. Those belong with the account holder, not an agency. Everything else in this proposal is built by us as normal.

ItemYou doWe do
Payment providerOpen the merchant account, enter the credentials in Shopify admin, run the first live transactionRecommend the provider, supply a step-by-step setup guide, and verify checkout end to end afterwards
Shipping zones & ratesEnter the zones and rate tiers in Shopify admin, and keep them updated when your carrier revises pricesTurn your current shipping terms into an exact zone-and-rate sheet, walk you through entering it, then test real destinations and weights at checkout

This does not reduce the quote: the configuration time is replaced by time writing the guide and verifying the result afterwards.

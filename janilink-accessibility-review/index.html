We reviewed your three stores against WCAG 2.2 Level AA on November 11, 2026. This document sets out what we found on each store, what we recommend fixing, and the hours and cost of each phase, so you can decide what to approve. Nothing has been changed on the stores.

1

What we found

All three stores work for most shoppers, but each has problems that stop or slow down someone using a keyboard or a screen reader. The most serious is on Tidywarehouse, where keyboard users cannot reach "Check out" in the cart drawer.

  • The themes are not Shopify's own. All three stores run paid Theme Store themes (Krank, Impulse and Athens) with our edits. Shopify tests its checkout and its free Dawn theme for accessibility, not these themes, so most fixes are theme work.
  • One issue blocks a purchase: the Tidywarehouse cart drawer (TW-01).
  • Menus and popups cause the biggest keyboard problems. Getting past the dropdown menus takes 127 Tab presses on Tidywarehouse and 139 on Janilink. The Janilink popup covers the page while focus stays behind it.
  • 14 of the 38 issues need no code. They are fixed with theme settings or content. Another 5 are partly fixed by a setting, and 19 need theme code, mostly small changes.
  • A good score is not the same as compliant. Lighthouse scores of 87 to 96 miss the cart drawer, popup and menu problems. Those came from our keyboard tests.
  • Already working: a skip link, a visible focus outline, labelled size options, clear sale prices, and the cart drawers on Cleaning Domain and Janilink. No store uses an accessibility overlay app, and we recommend not adding one, because it does not change the theme code.

1.1 How we tested

  • Tools: axe-core 4.14 set to WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 Level A and AA; Lighthouse 13.6; scripted keyboard tests in Chrome (Tab, Enter, Space, Esc); Chrome's accessibility tree for the names a screen reader announces; reflow at 320 px and 200% zoom. A person checked every screenshot, and 4 false automated results were left out.
  • Pages on each store: homepage with menus, search and popups; two collections (Carpet Cleaning Chemicals, Automatic Scrubbers) with filters; two products (one with a size option, one on sale with a photo gallery); search for "mop"; cart drawer and cart page; the first checkout step (no order placed); contact, newsletter and sign-in.
  • Product content: 33 products on Cleaning Domain, 56 on Tidywarehouse and 31 on Janilink, checked for alt text and description markup.
  • Screen sizes: desktop 1366 × 900 and iPhone 390 × 844.
  • Not covered yet: real screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver), which Phase 3 adds; signed-in account pages; PDF files; every product; testing with shoppers who have disabilities.

Where you are today

The store runs on Shopify (Dawn theme) and is bilingual (English / Spanish). 4

Suggested improvements

While looking at the current storefront we noticed four things worth improving. None of them are in the $1,350 above. Tell us which you want and we'll size them.

4.1 Homepage: make collection tiles look different from product cards

The collection tiles on the homepage (below) and your product cards are styled almost identically, so visitors can't tell a category from an item. We'd restyle the collection section so it reads as a category.

Current homepage collection tiles (Halflings, Humans, Tabaxi/Catfolk).

4.2 Product card: show the price and an Add to cart button

Cards should show the price and let shoppers add to cart straight from the listing. The example below, from another miniatures marketplace, shows the pattern: title, price, and a cart button on the card.

Reference: product card with price and add-to-cart (other site, for illustration).

4.3 Sub-menu: side (flyout) hover instead of the accordion

The current sub-menu opens as a long accordion inside the dropdown (screenshot below) and can feel broken on hover. A side flyout on hover is easier to use, especially for long lists like playable races.

Current Sci-Fi sub-menu: accordion inside the dropdown.

4.4 Collection pages: add filters

Add filters on collection pages, such as price and category, so shoppers can narrow down a large catalogue.

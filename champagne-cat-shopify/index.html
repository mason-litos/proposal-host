You're moving Champagne Cat from BigCommerce to Shopify and asked for two options: replicate the current site, or redesign it. We went through the live site page by page. The catalogue is small (22 products, 20 categories), so the move itself is straightforward. Most of the work is in how the store looks and reads. Below are both options, what we found on the current site, and the few things we need from you before the final number is locked (§4).

1

Two ways to make the move

1.1 Both options include

  • Setting up the Shopify store on Prestige or Impulse (§5), including theme configuration and navigation
  • Moving the catalogue and content: 22 products with their variants, 20 categories, 19 pages and 14 blog posts, plus redirects from the old URLs so search rankings and shared links keep working (§3)
  • Fixing the style bugs listed in §2 that the new theme's standard components solve
  • Full QA on desktop and mobile (cart, checkout and every page) before launch

1.2 Option 1 — Replicate ($1,500, 50h)

We rebuild the current site's pages, sections and content on Shopify in the same order and with the same messages. Because the new theme replaces BigCommerce's components, the broken and inconsistent parts in §2 are fixed along the way: the menu fits on one row, the sizes sit on one line, product cards line up, and the collection page has a filter sidebar. The homepage keeps its current structure, so the layout issues we describe in §2.2–2.5 stay as they are.

1.3 Option 2 — Redesign ($2,250, 75h) — recommended

Everything in Option 1, plus a dedicated redesign phase (+25h). We design and build a new homepage structure, a story section for Champagne Cat®, a new footer, a custom product card and a mobile header made for small screens, with a round of revisions. We recommend this option. The current homepage tries to tell the whole brand story at once, and as a result it doesn't guide visitors to buy. A redesign is the moment to fix that, rather than carrying it over to Shopify.

3

What changes moving off BigCommerce

This applies to both options.

3.1 Catalogue and content

From the site's sitemap: 22 products, 20 categories, 5 brands, 19 pages and 14 blog posts. Products move with their variants (color, size), prices and images. Categories become Shopify collections, brands become product vendors, and the "Happy Times" blog moves to a Shopify blog. We set up redirects from every old BigCommerce URL to its new Shopify address.

3.2 Reviews

Your products show star ratings from BigCommerce's built-in reviews. Shopify doesn't include reviews, so we install Judge.me (free plan available, paid plan $15/mo) and import your existing reviews.

3.3 Compare

Not carried over (§2). Both themes work without it.

3.4 Wishlist

The product page has "Add to Wish List" today. Neither Prestige nor Impulse includes a wishlist, so keeping it means adding a wishlist app. Tell us if your customers use it (§4).

Theme

You named two premium themes. Both handle everything in this proposal, including filters, a mega menu, color swatches and a slide-out cart. Either is a one-time purchase you make in your own Shopify admin:

ThemeLicenseFit for Champagne Cat
Impulse (Archetype Themes)$500Built for apparel stores that run promotions: quick buy, promo banners and countdowns. These fit your Seasonal Drops and Sale. Rated 94% positive from 1,301 reviews.
Prestige (Maestrooo)$400A quieter, editorial look with more room for story and imagery. It suits a brand-led site but is less promotional. Rated 91% positive from 869 reviews.

We'd lean towards Impulse, since your store is apparel-first with regular seasonal drops. If the story matters more to you than promotions, Prestige is the better fit. Shopify lets you try either theme for free before buying.

Who configures what

Payment and shipping are configured by you, in your own Shopify admin. We advise, document and verify, but we don't enter the credentials.

We do this on purpose. Connecting a payment provider means handling merchant credentials and API keys that give financial access to your business, and a shipping account carries your commercial rate agreement. Those belong with the account holder, not an agency. Everything else in this proposal is built by us as normal.

GoDaddy-to-Shopify Migration Proposal

Queen of Clothing — Shopify Migration Proposal

Client: Queen of Clothing — women's fashion & accessories retailer
Prepared by: LitOS/LitGroup
Date: August 10, 2026
1

Proposed scope

1.1 Platform

Migrate from GoDaddy to Shopify.

This proposal covers Shopify design & development only. Backend data migration (products, orders, customers, etc.) from GoDaddy to Shopify is not included — that's handled by a separate team, LitExtension (a sister brand). If needed, the product/order/customer volume to migrate would need to be shared with that team for their own estimate and quote.

1.2 Theme

Foundation theme for both options below: Ella — free through the Litos/HaloThemes partnership. Ella is purpose-built for fashion/apparel stores and natively covers several of the improvements in 1.3 without custom build: predictive search, product-card hover (image swap) with Quick View/Quick Add, on-page size and colour swatches (no dropdown), and collection filtering by size, colour, price, category, and availability.

1.3 Option 1 — Replicate & improve

Rebuilds the current site's structure and content on Shopify, with the following improvements:

  1. Search — overlay/popup search with predictive results as the customer types, or a narrower inline search field that no longer overlaps the logo.
  2. Contact — dedicated Contact page with the contact form, business info, and Google Map, moved off the homepage.
  3. Social icons — moved to the footer.
  4. Newsletter signup — moved to the footer, so it no longer interrupts the shopping journey on the homepage.
  5. Homepage layout — product sections moved higher up, plus new brand story/lifestyle/campaign-imagery sections and a collections section, so the homepage reads as more than a plain product catalog.
  6. Typography — a more modern, readable font.
  7. Product cards — hover effect, Quick View/Add to Cart, and an adjusted image ratio so the full product silhouette shows without cropping.
  8. Customer reviews — lighter background treatment.
  9. Product page — size options shown directly on the page instead of a dropdown.
  10. Collection page — filters for size, colour, price, category/type, and availability.

1.4 Option 2 — Redesign

Not bound by the current site's layout or structure — a modern Shopify store built around usability and conversion:

  1. Full UI/UX and information architecture redesign — modern, clean, user-friendly.
  2. Homepage, navigation, collection page, and product page rebuilt around user behaviour and business goals.
  3. Strong focus on product discovery and conversion — mobile-first, simple shopping flow, clear CTAs.
  4. Ella (1.2) as the foundation theme, customized to the brand and UX/UI requirements rather than replicating the old site's structure.
  5. Core brand identity — tone and messaging — preserved, with a modern visual direction suited to a fashion e-commerce store.
